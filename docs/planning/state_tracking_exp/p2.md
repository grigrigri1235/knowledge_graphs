# Part 2: Load the Saved Steps and Check the Counts

## Objective
Turn the saved conversations into one record per guarded TGR step (a step where the judge was called).

## Subtasks
1. **Read the file format** of `episodes.jsonl` (fields `executed`, `attempts`, `guard`) and write it down in
   `predicate_correctness/state_tracker/README.md`.
2. **Write `state_tracker/load_steps.py`.** One record per attempt that has a judge call:
   - run, domain, task, trial, attempt number;
   - history: the executed messages before the step (`executed[:executed_prefix_len]`). Blocked attempts are not in
     it, as in the guard;
   - the pending action: tool name and arguments;
   - the judge's value and evidence for each predicate;
   - the step's rules (circuit), the decision (allowed or blocked) and the violated rules.
3. **Check that the history is right:** on 5 steps, compare the history length with the guard's saved trace
   length.
4. **Check the tasks:** every task must be a train task in `splits.json`. If not, stop and report.
5. **Check the counts** against the brainstorm (sections 2.3 and 2.4):
   - judge values: 7,713 (gpt-6-luna agent) and 12,491 (gpt-5-nano agent);
   - blocked steps: 12 and 54 (gpt-6-luna; airline, retail), and 94 and 416 (gpt-5-nano).
6. **Save the records** to `output/steps_<run>_<domain>.jsonl`, and the counts to `output/p2_counts.md`.

## Deliverables
- `state_tracker/load_steps.py` and `state_tracker/README.md` (file format).
- `output/steps_*.jsonl` and `output/p2_counts.md`.

## Done when
- The counts match the brainstorm, or every difference is explained in `p2_counts.md`.
