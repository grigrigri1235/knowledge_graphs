Condition predicates. Declare every condition predicate in "predicates": a snake_case name, a precise self-contained description of when it is true (a reader who sees only the conversation and the pending action must be able to decide it), and "source", the evidence it is decided from: "call_arguments" (the arguments of the pending action), "tool_outputs" (results of earlier tool calls), "user_messages" (what the user said), "agent_messages" (what the agent told the user). A predicate can have several sources. Reuse one predicate wherever the meaning is the same. Predicate names must differ from action names. In "background_sentences", list the ids of policy sentences that state facts needed to decide your predicates (for example, the current date or time, or fixed amounts); the guardrail's fact checker receives their text.
